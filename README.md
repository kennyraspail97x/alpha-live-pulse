# Alpha Live

ALPHA PLACES — MASTER PROMPT FOR LOVABLE

Build a complete premium mobile-first platform called ALPHA PLACES.

ALPHA PLACES is a new category of product.

It combines:

real-world discovery

local social networking

geolocated Stories

geolocated Live video

interactive maps

restaurants

activities

events

nightlife

beaches

tourism

accommodation

booking

trip planning

travel budgeting

local business discovery

The first territory is Guadeloupe, but the architecture must be designed for international expansion.

This must NOT feel like:

Tripadvisor

Google Maps

Airbnb

Instagram

TikTok

Booking.com

ALPHA PLACES should combine some of the usefulness of those products while creating a completely new identity.



1 — THE CORE IDEA

ALPHA PLACES is the social network of the real world.

The application answers:

What is happening around me right now?

Where should I go?

What are people doing?

What places are alive right now?

What can I book?

What can I do tonight?

What can I afford during my trip?

The user should feel that the physical world around them has suddenly become visible and interactive.

FINAL INSTRUCTION

Do not reduce this into a tourism directory.

Do not reduce this into a social media clone.

Build ALPHA PLACES as a new social layer over the physical world.
